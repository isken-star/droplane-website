What DropLane is

DropLane records your drives automatically, keeps a note of what you earned and spent, and produces a mileage summary you can hand to an accountant or use when filing a tax return.

It is a record-keeping tool, not tax advice. The mileage rates built into the app are our best reading of published government rates at the date shown next to each one, and every one of them can be edited. Rates change, and eligibility rules differ by country and by circumstance — several countries, for instance, do not let a self-employed driver use a per-kilometre rate at all. What you put on your tax return is your responsibility, and an accountant is worth more than an app. We are not liable for tax owed, penalties or interest arising from figures produced by DropLane.

What we can and can't promise about accuracy

DropLane measures distance from your phone's location readings. GPS is very good, but it is not perfect: tunnels, multi-storey car parks, dense city centres, a phone left at home, a battery that dies mid-shift, or iOS deciding to be frugal with background activity can all cost a drive or part of one.

The app is built to lose as little as possible, but you should check your records rather than assume they are complete, especially over a tax year. You can add or edit a drive by hand at any time.

Subscription, trial and renewal

  • DropLane Pro is sold as a monthly or yearly subscription through the App Store, in your local currency.
  • New users get a free trial. Unless cancelled at least 24 hours before it ends, the trial rolls into a paid subscription.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. Your Apple Account is charged within 24 hours of the end of the current period.
  • Cancel any time in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the subscription runs to the end of the period you have paid for.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us, at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue refunds for App Store purchases, though we are happy to help you make the case.

Your data

Your drives, earnings and expenses are stored on your device, and in your own iCloud if you enable it. We have no server and no copy. That means we cannot recover your records if you lose your phone and had iCloud switched off — see the Privacy Policy for the full picture.
