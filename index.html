Questions couriers actually ask

Straight answers, from someone who does the job.

Isn't this just another mileage tracker?

It records mileage the same way, without a start button. The difference is what happens after: your takings go in, the fuel, parking and running costs come off, and the app tells you what the hour actually paid. A mileage figure on its own never told anyone whether the round was worth doing.

Does it drain the battery?

DropLane doesn't keep the GPS running all day. It sits quietly until it notices you've left where you parked, records while you're moving, and goes back to sleep when you stop.

Do I have to press start?

No — and there's no button to press. That's the whole point. If you have to remember, you'll forget on the day it matters.

What if I forget to sort a drive?

Nothing is lost. Unsorted drives wait on the Days screen, and the app nudges you rather than guessing for you.

Will my route history end up somewhere?

No. There's no account and no server. Everything lives on your iPhone, and backs up only to your own iCloud if you want it to.

I drive a bike, not a van.

That works. Cargo-bike miles are recorded the same way, and are just as claimable.

Is my country's mileage rate in there?

Seventeen countries have their official rate built in and kept current. Anywhere else, type your own rate once and the app does the maths.

Can I use it in kilometres?

Yes. Miles or kilometres, your own currency, and the fuel price you actually pay — all in Settings, on the first screen.
